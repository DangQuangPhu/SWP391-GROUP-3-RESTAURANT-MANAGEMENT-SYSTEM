# PCK-SCP: User Personas

This document contains **two diverse user personas** representing the target users for the **Phūrai Collaborative Kitchen & Service Coordination Portal (PCK-SCP)**. These personas illustrate the range of backgrounds, skill levels, motivations, and operational constraints faced by restaurant staff.

---

## 1. Project Title
**Phūrai Collaborative Kitchen & Service Coordination Portal (PCK-SCP)**

---

## 2. Persona 1: The Fast-Paced FOH Server (Front-of-House)

### User Profile: Minh "Sunny" Tran
* **Role:** Lead Waiter / Shift Captain
* **Age:** 22 years old
* **Education:** Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Hospitality Management (Part-time)
* **Work Context:** Works part-time shifts during peak hours (dinner rush & weekends). Constantly moving between tables, the kitchen counter, and the cashier stand.

### Demographics & Lifestyle
* **Tech Literacy:** High. Native smartphone and tablet user. Uses mobile banking, social media, and food delivery apps daily.
* **Environment:** Extremely noisy, busy, and chaotic. Minh is constantly interrupted by customers while inputting data.
* **Working Hours:** 20 hours per week, mostly late shifts (5:00 PM – 11:00 PM).

### Motivations & Needs
* **Fast Table Turnover:** Needs to seat, take orders from, and check out parties as fast as possible to maximize tips and shift revenue.
* **Error Prevention:** Needs clear alerts if a dish is running low on stock to prevent placing invalid orders that he has to apologize for later.
* **Seamless Splits:** Needs a quick, foolproof way to split bills by item or seat, as friend groups frequently ask for separate payments.
* **Food State Visibility:** Needs to know the second a hot dish is ready to be picked up from the kitchen so it doesn't get cold on the counter.

### System Barriers & Facilitators
* **Barriers:** 
  * **Distraction:** Easily distracted by guest requests mid-order. If a screen requires too many steps or lacks auto-saves, he loses cart data.
  * **Single-Handed Use:** Often carries trays or clean glasses, leaving only one hand free to interact with tablet controls.
* **Facilitators:**
  * **UI Familiarity:** Learns digital layouts instantly. Prefers search filters, gesture swipes, and dark mode screens.
  * **Alert Tolerance:** Highly responsive to push notifications, vibrating alarms, and haptic keyboard responses.

## 3. Persona 2: The Traditional BOH Head Chef (Back-of-House)

### User Profile: Chef Hung Nguyen
* **Role:** BOH Head Line Cook / Kitchen Supervisor
* **Age:** 48 years old
* **Education:** Vocational Culinary Training Certificate (1998)
* **Work Context:** Manages food preparation lines at the hot stove station. Spends 10 hours a day standing in a hot, humid, and greasy environment.

### Demographics & Lifestyle
* **Tech Literacy:** Low-to-medium. Uses a basic smartphone for calls and texting, but is uncomfortable with complex settings, multi-layer menus, and software updates.
* **Environment:** High heat, humidity, steam, and safety hazards (knives, fire). Greasy, wet fingers.
* **Working Hours:** Full-time (50 hours per week).

### Motivations & Needs
* **Clear Task Priority:** Needs to see exactly what dishes to cook first based on order queue times and VIP priorities.
* **Allergen Alert Visibility:** Needs critical dietary specifications (e.g., *"NO NUTS - SEVERE ALLERGY"*) highlighted in large, high-contrast text that is impossible to overlook.
* **Durable Interaction:** Needs big, simple touch targets on screen controls because his hands are often wet, gloved, or oily.
* **Zero Admin Overhead:** Wants to spend his time cooking, not pressing buttons or troubleshooting terminal connectivity.

### System Barriers & Facilitators
* **Barriers:**
  * **Physical Interface Issues:** Small buttons and sliders fail to register when touched with wet or gloved hands.
  * **Visual Impairment:** Has mild presbyopia (far-sightedness) and struggles to read small font sizes on monitors placed 2 meters away from the stove.
  * **Frustration:** Easily gets frustrated by pop-up modals, multi-step confirmations, and system notifications that block his primary queue view.
* **Facilitators:**
  * **Process-Driven Focus:** Highly organized and follows repetitive, linear processes. If the KDS mimics a physical ticket rail (moving tickets left-to-right), he will adopt it quickly.
  * **Sound cues:** Responds well to loud auditory beeps when new orders arrive, letting him know when to look up at the screen.
