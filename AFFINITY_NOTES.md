# User Needs Assessment: Affinity Notes Data Extraction

This document compiles the data extracted from two user interview and observation sessions regarding Spotify podcast management.

---

## 1. Project Title
**User Needs Assessment: Managing Saved Podcasts on the Spotify Mobile App**

---

## Section 1: Mini-Project Description

Spotify is a leading global audio streaming service with over 500 million active users. While the platform was originally built for music, it has integrated podcasts as a core feature of its mobile application. Within the "Your Library" tab, users can follow podcast shows, save individual episodes, download audio files for offline listening, and view automatically compiled playlists such as "New Episodes" and "Your Episodes." As podcast catalogs grow, users are increasingly using Spotify as a daily podcast management application, which requires sophisticated library sorting, storage administration, and consumption status tracking.

This needs assessment project is designed to understand how users interact with these podcast management interfaces. Specifically, the project will look at how users organize their upcoming listening queues and manage their device storage space. This scope is appropriate for two hour-long usability interviews because it focuses on a specific, self-contained set of features within the larger Spotify app. During the sessions, the researcher can observe users interacting with their "Your Library" tab, watch them attempt to sort or filter their podcast episodes, and listen to them describe their mental models for organizing audio content.

## Section 3: Direct Quotations from Participants

* **Note 1 (P1):** "I download episodes before flying, but then I can never find where the downloaded list is hidden, so I end up just streaming them on cellular data by accident."
* **Note 2 (P1):** "It is incredibly annoying that I have to click three separate buttons just to delete one single episode that I’ve already finished listening to."
* **Note 3 (P2):** "I wish there was a simple 'Select All' or checkmark system so I could clean up my download list. Right now, it takes me five minutes of tapping just to clear space on my phone."
* **Note 4 (P2):** "The app keeps telling me I have new episodes, but it lists everything together, and I cannot easily see which ones are half-played versus completely unplayed."
* **Note 5 (P1):** "I hoarded over 40 episodes in my queue, and when I tried to drag the bottom one to the top, it took me forever because it didn't auto-scroll fast enough."

---

## Section 4: Observations and Interpretations

* **Note 6 (P1):** Observed the participant tapping back and forth between "Your Episodes" and the "Downloaded" filter button three times before locating their offline files.
* **Note 7 (P1):** Interpret that the lack of clear, distinct icons for "offline status" vs. "saved status" leads the user to assume that saving an episode automatically downloads it.
* **Note 8 (P2):** Observed the participant struggling to find a bulk edit mode; they tapped and held on an episode card expecting a checkmark selection layout to appear, but it only opened an options modal.
* **Note 9 (P2):** Interpret that users expect standard mobile operating system behaviors (like long-press to select multiple items) to apply to Spotify's list layouts.
* **Note 10 (P1):** Observed the participant manually scrolling down a queue of 25 episodes to find a specific show, failing to notice the small search bar at the very top of the list.

---

## Section 5: Factual Statements (Non-Quotations)

* **Note 11 (P1):** The participant listens to podcasts for approximately 2 hours daily during their train commute.
* **Note 12 (P1):** The participant currently has 8.4 Gigabytes of local device storage occupied by downloaded Spotify podcast episodes.
* **Note 13 (P2):** The participant follows a total of 18 different podcast shows on Spotify, with 6 of those shows updating weekly.
* **Note 14 (P2):** The participant's mobile phone runs on iOS 17, and they use the latest version of the Spotify app available on the App Store.
* **Note 15 (P2):** The participant has never successfully used the "Hide Played Episodes" toggle option because they did not know it existed in the settings menu.

---

## Section 6: Additional General Affinity Notes

* **Note 16 (P1):** The participant prefers listening to video podcasts but disables the video stream to save battery power when traveling.
* **Note 17 (P1):** The participant was able to successfully rearrange their play queue after discovering the drag-and-drop handles on the right edge of the list.
* **Note 18 (P2):** The participant frequently switches between Spotify on their desktop computer and mobile phone, and notices that their custom podcast queue order does not sync between the two devices.
* **Note 19 (P2):** The participant expressed a desire for a "smart auto-delete" option that automatically removes downloaded files 24 hours after they are marked as completed.
* **Note 20 (P1):** The participant uses the "Your Library" filter tabs to toggle between music and podcasts, stating that this division is the most useful organization feature on the landing screen.
