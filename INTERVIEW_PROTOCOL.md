# User Needs Assessment: Interview Protocol

## 1. Project Title
**User Needs Assessment: Managing Saved Podcasts on the Spotify Mobile App**

---

## Section 1: Mini-Project Description

Spotify is a leading global audio streaming service with over 500 million active users. While the platform was originally built for music, it has integrated podcasts as a core feature of its mobile application. Within the "Your Library" tab, users can follow podcast shows, save individual episodes, download audio files for offline listening, and view automatically compiled playlists such as "New Episodes" and "Your Episodes." As podcast catalogs grow, users are increasingly using Spotify as a daily podcast management application, which requires sophisticated library sorting, storage administration, and consumption status tracking.

This needs assessment project is designed to understand how users interact with these podcast management interfaces. Specifically, the project will look at how users organize their upcoming listening queues and manage their device storage space. This scope is appropriate for two hour-long usability interviews because it focuses on a specific, self-contained set of features within the larger Spotify app. During the sessions, the researcher can observe users interacting with their "Your Library" tab, watch them attempt to sort or filter their podcast episodes, and listen to them describe their mental models for organizing audio content.

---

## Section 2: Overarching Research Questions

1. **Overarching Question 1:** How do frequent podcast listeners navigate the Spotify mobile interface to filter, sort, and organize their active listening queue?
2. **Overarching Question 2:** What strategies and UI barriers do users experience when attempting to track, download, and clean up local storage space occupied by podcast episodes?

## Section 3: Interview Introduction Script

**Introduction Protocol (Read to participant at the start of the session):**

*"Hello! My name is Dang Quang Phu, and I want to thank you so much for taking the time to meet with me today. In this session, I am conducting a research study to understand how people manage their saved and downloaded podcast episodes within the Spotify mobile application. The interview will take approximately 60 minutes to complete.*

*During this session, I will ask you some questions about your podcast habits and then observe you as you perform a few common tasks on your Spotify app. There are no right or wrong answers, and we are not testing your skills—we are evaluating how the app fits your needs.*

*Your participation today is completely voluntary. You are free to skip any question you do not wish to answer, and you may stop the interview at any point. Everything we discuss today, as well as any screen and audio recordings, will be kept strictly confidential. The recordings and notes will be used solely for my academic course analysis and will be securely deleted once the project is graded.*

*Do you have any questions before we begin? If you are ready, I will start the recording now."*

## Section 4: Interview Question Section

### Part A: Warm-up and Listening Habits
1. **Core Question:** Can you describe your typical podcast listening routine?
   * *Follow-up:* What types of shows do you listen to, and what times of day do you usually listen?
   * *Follow-up:* Do you listen primarily when connected to the internet, or do you download episodes for offline listening?
2. **Core Question:** Walk me through how you decide what podcast episode to listen to next when you open the Spotify app.
   * *Follow-up:* Where do you go in the app to find your saved episodes?
   * *Follow-up:* How do you keep track of episodes you haven't finished yet?

### Part B: Library Navigation and Organization (Observational Tasks)
3. **Core Question (Observational):** Please open your Spotify app, go to your library, and show me the last podcast episode you saved but haven't listened to yet.
   * *Follow-up:* What filters or sorting options did you use to locate it?
   * *Follow-up:* How clear is it to you which episodes are "new" versus "in-progress" on this screen?
4. **Core Question (Observational):** Tell me about a specific time when you wanted to organize your podcast queue for a long trip or commute. How did you do that in Spotify?
   * *Follow-up:* Can you show me how you would add three specific episodes to your queue right now?
   * *Follow-up:* How easy or difficult is it to change the order of the episodes in that queue?

### Part C: Storage Management & Housekeeping (Observational Tasks)
5. **Core Question (Observational):** Show me how you would check which podcast episodes are currently downloaded onto your device.
   * *Follow-up:* How do you distinguish between an episode that is just "saved" in your library list and one that is physically "downloaded" to your phone?
   * *Follow-up:* What visual cues do you look for to confirm a download is complete?
6. **Core Question (Observational):** Walk me through how you would delete five podcast episodes that you have already finished listening to.
   * *Follow-up:* Is there a way to select all of them and delete them at once? Show me how you would attempt that.
   * *Follow-up:* How do you feel about the number of steps required to keep your library clean?
