# Phūrai Restaurant Loyalty & Voucher System Survey Design

## 1. Research Topic
The primary objective of this survey is to measure **customer perceptions, user experience, engagement willingness, and functional satisfaction** regarding the newly introduced **Loyalty Points & Voucher Redemption System** at Phūrai Restaurant. The survey aims to evaluate whether the point-to-monetary value ratio is appealing, if the checkouts are seamless, and which features drive customer retention.

## 2. Respondent Introduction & Survey Description
Thank you for dining at Phūrai Restaurant! We have recently launched our new Loyalty Points & Reward Catalog to show appreciation for our valued guests. Please take 3–5 minutes to share your honest feedback about your experience earning and redeeming reward points; your responses will directly help us refine and improve our dining rewards program.

---

## 3. Survey Questions

### Question 1: System Enrollment
* **Type:** Nominal Closed-Ended (Dichotomous)
* **Question:** Have you registered for the Phūrai Loyalty Points program?
* **Options:**
  - [ ] Yes
  - [ ] No

### Question 2: Usage Frequency
* **Type:** Ordinal Closed-Ended (Frequency Likert)
* **Question:** How often do you check your loyalty point balance on the portal?
* **Options:**
  - [ ] Daily
  - [ ] Weekly
  - [ ] Monthly
  - [ ] Only when preparing to checkout or pay a bill
  - [ ] Never

### Question 3: Earning Points Perceived Value
* **Type:** Ordinal Closed-Ended (Agreement Likert)
* **Question:** "Earning 1 Loyalty Point for every 10,000 VND spent provides fair value for my purchases."
* **Options:**
  - [ ] Strongly Agree
  - [ ] Agree
  - [ ] Neutral
  - [ ] Disagree
  - [ ] Strongly Disagree

### Question 4: Primary Earning Method
* **Type:** Nominal Closed-Ended (Multiple Choice)
* **Question:** Which reservation or payment method do you primarily use to accumulate points?
* **Options:**
  - [ ] Dine-In Checkout Payments
  - [ ] Table Preorders / Deposit Bookings
  - [ ] Special Event Bookings
  - [ ] Promo campaigns and registration bonuses

### Question 5: Catalog Appeal
* **Type:** Ordinal Closed-Ended (Rating Scale)
* **Question:** How appealing do you find the current selection of vouchers in our Rewards Catalog?
* **Options:**
  - (1) Not Appealing At All
  - (2) Slightly Appealing
  - (3) Neutral
  - (4) Very Appealing
  - (5) Extremely Appealing

### Question 6: Redemption Process Experience
* **Type:** Ordinal Closed-Ended (Agreement Likert)
* **Question:** "Exchanging my points for a voucher code and applying it at checkout was quick and straightforward."
* **Options:**
  - [ ] Strongly Agree
  - [ ] Agree
  - [ ] Neutral
  - [ ] Disagree
  - [ ] Strongly Disagree
  - [ ] I have not redeemed a voucher yet

### Question 7: Countdown Timers
* **Type:** Nominal Closed-Ended (Multiple Choice)
* **Question:** Which visual indicator on the "My Vouchers" page did you find most useful?
* **Options:**
  - [ ] The real-time countdown clock (HH:MM:SS)
  - [ ] The color-coded progress bar (Green/Yellow/Red indicator)
  - [ ] Standard expiration dates in static text format
  - [ ] None of the above

### Question 8: Points Clawback Transparency
* **Type:** Nominal Closed-Ended (Multiple Choice)
* **Question:** How clear is the policy that points earned from an order will be deducted (clawed back) if that order is refunded?
* **Options:**
  - [ ] Completely clear and expected
  - [ ] Somewhat clear, but needs more documentation
  - [ ] Unclear, I was not aware of this policy

* **Type:** Open-Ended (Qualitative feedback)

* **Type:** Ordinal Closed-Ended (Likert Scale)
* **Question:** How likely is the Phūrai Loyalty Points program to influence you to dine with us again in the future?
* **Options:**
  - [ ] Extremely Likely
